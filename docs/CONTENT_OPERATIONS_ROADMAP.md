## Content quality requirements

Before any guide moves to `published`, it should include:

- Direct answer
- Safety boundary if relevant
- Practical checklist or next steps
- Source anchors
- Last reviewed date
- Related template or internal link where possible
- Professional-help triggers for sensitive topics

## Expert review targets

Prioritize expert review for:

- Bathroom safety
- Fall risk
- Grab bars
- Shower chairs and transfer benches
- Bed rails
- Medication management
- Dementia-related safety
- Ramps and stair lifts
- Medical alert systems

Potential reviewer types:

- Occupational therapist
- Pharmacist
- Certified Aging-in-Place Specialist
- Geriatric care manager
- Licensed contractor/home modification specialist

## Monetization guardrails

Do not add affiliate links until:

- Affiliate disclosure is live
- Product review methodology exists
- Product criteria are transparent
- The page helps readers even without buying
- Claims are sourced to manufacturer or reliable guidance

## Near-term goal

Build a useful 20-resource foundation:

- 10 source-backed guides
- 6 printable templates
- 4 roadmap section pages

This gives the site enough depth to start earning trust before pursuing product reviews, affiliate content, newsletter growth, or expert partnerships.
