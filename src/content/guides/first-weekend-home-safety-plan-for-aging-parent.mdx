## Direct answer

The first weekend should focus on obvious hazards and essential information, not a full home remodel. Clear walking paths, improve lighting, check the bathroom, organize emergency contacts and medications, and write down what needs professional review.

## Before you start

Talk with the older adult first. The goal is to support safety and independence, not take over the home. Ask what feels difficult, what feels embarrassing, and what they want help with first.

## Hour 1: Walk the main paths

Start with the routes used every day:

- Bed to bathroom
- Favorite chair to kitchen
- Kitchen to entry door
- Entry door to car or mailbox
- Bedroom to phone or alert device

Write down clutter, cords, loose rugs, poor lighting, narrow pathways, and awkward furniture placement.

## Hour 2: Check the bathroom

Look for:

- Slippery tub or shower surfaces
- Loose bath mats
- Poor lighting
- Toiletries that require bending or reaching
- Towel bars or fixtures being used for support
- Trouble stepping into or out of the tub or shower

Do not install random products just to feel productive. If grab bars, shower seating, or toilet-height changes may be needed, write down the concern and get qualified help.

## Hour 3: Organize emergency basics

Create a simple emergency sheet with:

- Primary emergency contact
- Backup contact
- Primary care clinician
- Pharmacy
- Preferred hospital
- Allergies
- Current medications
- Home access notes for trusted helpers

## Hour 4: Review medications and appointments

Do not change medications. Instead, organize information:

- Current medication list
- Dose and schedule
- Prescriber
- Pharmacy
- Refill questions
- Recent changes
- Questions for a pharmacist or clinician

## Hour 5: Decide what needs professional help

Flag anything involving:

- Falls or near-falls
- Mobility changes
- Memory or judgment concerns
- Medication mistakes
- Bathing or toileting difficulty
- Stairs, ramps, grab bars, or major modifications
- Recent hospital or rehab discharge

## Weekend output

By the end of the weekend, aim to have:

- Main walking paths cleared
- Basic lighting gaps noted
- Bathroom concerns listed
- Emergency contact sheet started
- Medication list started
- First five action items written down
- Professional questions listed
