# Aging at Home Lab

**Practical checklists, product tests, home-safety guides, and care-organization systems for families helping aging parents stay safer at home.**

This repository is the operating base for the Aging at Home Lab publishing project.

Production URL:

```text
https://rise-unplugged.vercel.app/
```

## Mission

Aging at Home Lab exists to help adult children, family caregivers, and older adults make safer, clearer, better-organized decisions about aging in place.

The goal is not to publish generic advice. Every major guide should include practical next steps, credible sourcing, original templates or visuals, and clear boundaries around medical, legal, financial, and safety topics.

## Core content pillars

1. **Home Safety** — room-by-room aging-in-place checklists, fall-risk reduction, bathroom safety, stairs, lighting, flooring, and emergency readiness.
2. **Caregiver Organization** — caregiver binders, medication lists, appointment notes, emergency contact sheets, sibling coordination, and care calendars.
3. **Senior Tech** — medical alert systems, fall detection, smart home sensors, monitoring options, privacy tradeoffs, medication reminders, and caregiver apps.
4. **Home Modifications** — grab bars, ramps, stair lifts, shower conversions, contractor questions, accessibility upgrades, and cost planning.
5. **Decision Guides** — when to call a professional, when home care may be needed, how to compare agencies, and how families can plan before a crisis.
6. **Templates & Tools** — printable PDFs, checklists, worksheets, decision trees, comparison tables, and caregiver planning documents.

## Editorial principles

- Be practical before clever.
- Cite credible sources.
- Avoid medical, legal, or financial advice unless clearly bounded and reviewed.
- Add original usefulness to every important article.
- Disclose affiliate relationships clearly.
- Explain when readers should call a professional.
- Keep guides updated and mark last-reviewed dates.
